## Ordering
- Order on the website or through the hotline.
- Every order gets an order number like NG-123456 by SMS.
- Payment: cash on delivery, bKash, Nagad, card (Visa/Mastercard).
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are handed to the courier (usually within 24 hours).

## Delivery
- Inside Dhaka: 1–2 working days, delivery charge 60 taka.
- Outside Dhaka: 3–5 working days, delivery charge 120 taka.
- Free delivery on orders above 5,000 taka.
- Delivery partners: our own riders in Dhaka, a courier service outside Dhaka.
- Customers get the rider's phone number by SMS on the delivery day.
- If an order is late by more than 2 days beyond the promised time, the delivery charge is refunded.

## Returns and refunds
- Return within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused, in its original box, with all accessories.
- Damaged, defective or wrong item: return within 7 days even if opened; we pay the return delivery.
- Change of mind: customer pays the return delivery charge.
- Refunds go back the way the customer paid (bKash/Nagad/card); cash-on-delivery refunds go to bKash or Nagad.
- Refund time: 3–5 working days after we receive the returned item.
- Not returnable: opened earbuds/headphones (hygiene), software, gift cards — unless defective.

## Warranty
- Phones: 1 year official warranty. Laptops: 1–2 years depending on brand. Accessories: 6 months.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not physical damage, water damage or unauthorised repair.
- Warranty claims: bring the product and invoice to the service centre (House 12, Road 5, Dhanmondi, Dhaka),
  or send it by courier; repair takes 7–15 working days.

## What the agent can do
- Log a complaint or request (late delivery, wrong/damaged item, refund follow-up, warranty) against the
  customer's order number. The right team then calls the customer back within 24 hours.
- Arrange a return pickup for wrong, damaged or defective items (free of charge), once it has the order number.
- Offer a call-back from a senior colleague when the customer is very upset or the issue is not solved.
- Pass compliments and feedback on to the team (riders, service centre).
- Cancel an order that has not yet gone to the courier, once it has the order number.

## What the agent cannot do
- It cannot see live order status, payments or stock in this demo: it asks for the order number, logs the
  issue and tells the customer the team will call back - it never invents a status, a date or stock.
- It never promises things not written here (no extra discounts, no exceptions to the policy).
- For abuse, legal threats, or anything it cannot solve: offer a call-back from a senior colleague.
